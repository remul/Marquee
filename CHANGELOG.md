# Changelog

## 0.2.2

- **Outfit titles for outfits the game puts on by itself** are now worn with your next key press,
  or click on the game world or an action button, instead of asking first. Asking is still in
  Options (**When the game changes your outfit**). Characters that already have a setting keep it.
- **Simpler README**, written for players.

## 0.2.1

- **Long titles fit better** in the Titles list and the outfit title picker: titles too long for a
  card get a slightly smaller font, and take the star's space while the star doesn't show. While it
  shows, a long title ends in "..." instead of changing size.

## 0.2.0

- **Outfit titles for outfits the game puts on by itself** (situations): Marquee asks whether to
  wear the outfit's title. In Options (**When the game changes your outfit**) your next key press,
  or click on the game world or an action button, can wear it instead, or the title stays as it
  is. Never in combat: the title waits until the fight ends.
- **The gear** next to the search box is grey, and gold while you point at it or its menu is open.

## 0.1.2

- **37 more titles sorted** into the filter's activities (PvP, quests, reputation, Delves, pet
  battles, world events, Skyriding, exploration and expansion features); fewer titles are left
  under Other.

## 0.1.1

- **Outfit titles follow Save Outfit**: saving changes to an outfit puts it on, so its title is
  worn too.
- **Choosing a title for the outfit you're wearing** wears it right away, instead of the next time
  you put the outfit on.

## 0.1.0

- First version.
- **Search and filters** above the Titles list (the Filter button, then the search box below it) in the Character window: search your titles by
  name, and filter them by **expansion** and by **activity** (raids, dungeons, Delves, PvP, world
  events, reputation, quests, professions, and more). Filters are saved; the Filter button's reset
  shows them all again.
- **Title cards**: the titles in the list are rounded cards like Lineup's, with a bit of space
  between them. The chosen title has a gold outline instead of a check mark, so titles have the
  whole width. Lines divide No Title and the Random rows, your favourites, and your other titles.
- **Favourites**: click the star on a title (it shows while you point at the title) to make it a
  favourite. Favourites come right after "No Title", before your other titles.
- **Gear menu** next to the search box: **Own favorites for this character** lets a character keep
  its own favorite titles instead of the shared ones (each character starts with the shared ones).
  **Open Options** at the bottom opens Marquee's settings for all characters in Blizzard's
  Options (AddOns tab).
- **Random titles**: **Random** and **Random Favorite** below "No Title" wear a random title (or
  favorite) when clicked; `/marquee random` and `/marquee random favorite` do the same, e.g. from a
  macro.
- **A title for each outfit**: in the transmog window, **Title** at the top right of the character
  preview shows the outfit's title; click it to choose one (or No Title, Random, Random Favorite)
  from a list with the same filters, search and favourites as the Titles list. Putting the outfit on wears it: clicking its icon
  in the transmog window, or using the outfit on Blizzard's action bars (click or key). The
  outfit's tooltip shows its title. Outfits the game switches to by itself don't change the title
  (the game only allows that from a click or key press).
- `/marquee` opens the Character window on the Titles list.
